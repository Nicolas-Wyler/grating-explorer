# Changelog

## 1.3.3 — 6 November 2026

Installable offline browser app with a dedicated icon, automatic updates, persistent result reuse and history, restored session state, and library backup/import. Uses a worker and Pyodide for local scientific calculations. This release adds non-destructive transfer of previous Mac-app results into the browser library.

## 1.2.0 — 6 November 2026

Mac app: added persistent results, reuse, progress and ETA, history, storage indicator and session restoration.

## 1.1.0 — 6 November 2026

Mac app: adjustable linear ceiling based on the current view maximum.

## 1.0.0 — 6 November 2026

Initial local point-source heatmap and annular-integration interface.
